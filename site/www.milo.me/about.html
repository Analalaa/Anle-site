About me

Stay curious. Give things time to grow.

Have you eaten?! I am Anle, also Milo An. You can also call me Zisu (子溯). I was born in 2001, an ENP-type Aries. I keep this site as a quiet place for writing, photography, and personal notes.

I am drawn to imaginative ideas and enjoy refining them into real things. I want to build practical tools that help myself and others improve efficiency, so life can return to people themselves. I am working to build my own way of living.

I believe the most important task is to play: to think about what I truly want to play with, not only what can make money. I believe a playful mindset is what lets a person do something well and eventually make money from it.

Many things grow rather than being designed. The timing may simply not have arrived yet, so short-term thinking should not wear down long-term ambition.

I sometimes write articles, and I make small tools and experiments from things I need and things I love.

Outside of creating, I love sports, photography, and travel. Badminton was my favorite sport during graduate school. I want to experience baseball, golf, and dance. I think I have good body coordination and am good at visual movement. You can find my photography works above. I also like literature and films. I am learning instruments too, with the goal of learning one new instrument each year.

So I hope you can see that I am a person who believes in long-termism. Ten years from now, I will still want to see beautiful landscapes, cross mountains and rivers, and experience snow peaks, lakes, and nature. I will still love music, use AI to create and make things real, enjoy competitive sports, value sincere conversations between people, and keep recording life through images, sound, and words.

People should receive joy and strength from what they love, instead of spending all their joy and strength just to keep loving it.
