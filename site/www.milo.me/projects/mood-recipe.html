Mood Recipe

Add a moment to the music.

Treat a feeling as an ingredient, and music as a recipe that keeps growing. Describe a scent, a texture, a memory, or a kind of light — or upload an image — and let that moment become part of the music.

  • Words, or a picture

    Describe a mood or a sensory memory, or upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image as an emotional input.

  • Build a piece, layer by layer

    Each entry adds an independent musical layer alongside earlier sounds, gradually changing the rhythm, timbre, and atmosphere.

  • Keep an emotional score

    Revisit submitted words, images, and mood labels in the layer map and emotional archive, or remove an entry and its musical layer.

Interpreting image content requires an enabled vision service. When it is unavailable or analysis fails, images use a default mood. A public link is being prepared.
