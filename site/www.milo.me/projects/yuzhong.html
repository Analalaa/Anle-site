Feather Mound Project

Off the court, a new story began.

My roleFounder · Planning · Organizing

In June 2026, I founded and completed Feather Mound Project in Zhengzhou. Using discarded shuttlecocks and their tubes, the series grew from a conversation about unwanted objects into collaborative making and an exhibition.

  • Why I started it

    Before leaving Zhengzhou, I wanted to make something with the people here. Discarded shuttlecocks and empty tubes became our starting point: a way to see value in overlooked objects and invite people without an art background to create.

  • What we did

    We collected materials and invited participants to meet at Shiyi Art Museum in Ruiguang Creative Factory. We sketched, exchanged ideas, and worked individually or in small groups. The hollow tubes, damaged feathers, and traces of use led to installations, sound, light, and interactive work, which we brought together in a shared exhibition space.

  • What remained

    The gatherings and exhibition concluded in June, turning discarded shuttlecocks and tubes into work people could view and interact with. The project also produced interactive pages including a pixel photo booth and a tube-themed pixel world, where photographs and text became part of the creative process.
