Public co-creation / Material reuse

Feather Mound Project

The game ends. The story can continue.

My roleFounder · Planning · Organizing

Where do worn shuttlecocks and empty tubes go after a game? That question led me to start Feather Mound Project (羽冢企划). A tube’s shape, hollow interior, and traces of use can all become starting points for making. Through gatherings and shared exhibition planning, we explore how discarded objects can connect people, places, and imagination.

  • A second life for materials

    Reuse begins with more than a new function. What an object held, how it wore out, and why it was discarded become questions for making. Crafts, installations, light, sound, and games offer different directions to explore.

  • Meet first. Make room for ideas.

    No art background, badminton experience, or finished work is required. Introductions and a short project sharing lead into open sketching and conversation, giving even an unfinished thought a place to begin.

  • Bring ideas into a shared space

    I founded, planned, and organized the series, connecting participants, materials, and a venue. Preparation brings together invitations, tube collection, creative proposals, and discussions of space, equipment, and exhibition materials.

Where do the materials go after the exhibition?

This question has been part of the project from the beginning. Being seen again is one step. Continued use, circulation, or recycling determines whether an object truly gets another life. It is a question I want every gathering and creative experiment to leave open.
