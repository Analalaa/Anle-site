Notes / Android app

Recoding · 随记

Catch a thought. Give it room to grow.

Download for Android ↓

Suiji is a local notebook that starts with writing. Open a fresh page, skip the title if you like, and put a thought down without signing in. Organize your little world with folders and tags whenever you are ready.

  • Open and write

    Start with the body, save automatically, and undo accidental text edits.

  • Keep the picture and the sound

    Add several photos, zoom in to explore them, or record and play back original audio.

  • Find it and take it with you

    Use folders, tags, and search, or export a ZIP containing your text, photographs, and audio.

A test release for Android 8.0+. Notes stay on your device. Offline speech-to-text is not yet available; real-device experience still needs further validation.

Try Suiji on your phone

Android 8.0+ · 0.6.1-device · 1.28 MiB · Test release

Download APK ↓
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the APK, or transfer it from your computer.
  2. Open the downloaded file, follow Android’s installation prompt, and launch Suiji.
  3. Write a few words, add photos, or record a sound. Export important notes from the app to keep a backup.

For Android phones. It does not run on iPhone or in a web browser.
