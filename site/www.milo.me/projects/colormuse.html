Image / Color tool

Color Muse

Borrow a palette. Tell your own story.

Try it online ↗

Sometimes the first thing I fall for in a photograph is its color. Color Muse turns that instinct into a small tool: give it a reference, and see what palette your own image might grow into.

  • Start with a palette

    Upload a photo and choose a built-in style or your own reference image.

  • Find the right balance

    Adjust the transfer strength and compare the before and after.

  • Keep creating

    Download the image or export a .CUBE LUT to continue in another color-grading tool.

Open the website to get started. Images are processed in your browser without being uploaded to a server.
